# Roasted Cabbage #

Makes About 8 Servings

## Ingredients ##

- 1 Large Head Green Cabbage (about 2 1/4 lb, 1000 g), or red cabbage
- 2 T Olive Oil
- Salt and Pepper

## Instructions ##

1. Preheat oven (or air fryer) to 400°F.
2. Cut the cabbage into bite-size pieces (or wedges), discarding the core.
3. Toss with olive oil, salt, and pepper and spread out on a sheet pan in a single layer.
4. Roast for 20-25 minutes, flipping halfway, until tender with crispy brown edges.
