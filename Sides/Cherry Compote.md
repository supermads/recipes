# Cherry Compote #

Makes About 6 Servings

## Ingredients ##

- 2 c Cherries, pitted (fresh or frozen) ⚠️
- 1 Cup Mandarin Oranges in Juice (about 4 oz), with the juice ⚠️
- 1/4 c Sugar ⚠️

## Instructions ##

1. Add everything to a small saucepan over medium heat.
2. Simmer, stirring occasionally, for 10-15 minutes, until the cherries break down and the juices get syrupy.
3. Cool slightly before serving. It thickens as it cools.

## Notes ##

- Adapted from *The Official Stardew Valley Cookbook*, using mandarins in juice. Great on [Rice Pudding](../dessert/Rice%20Pudding.md), pancakes, or ice cream.
