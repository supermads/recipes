# Szechuan Sweet Potato Fries #

Makes About 4 Servings

## Ingredients ##

- 1 lb Sweet Potatoes
- 1 T Olive Oil
- 1 T Cornstarch ⚠️
- 1 t Garlic Powder ⚠️
- 1 t Szechuan Peppercorns, toasted and ground ⚠️
- Pinch of Black Pepper
- Salt to Taste

## Instructions ##

1. Cut sweet potatoes into even, 1/4"-thick fries. Soak in a bowl of cold water for at least 30 minutes, or overnight.
2. Preheat oven to 425°F and line a large sheet pan with parchment.
3. Drain the fries and pat them very dry with a clean towel.
4. Toss the fries with olive oil in a dry bowl.
5. Whisk together cornstarch, garlic powder, Szechuan pepper, and black pepper. Sprinkle over the fries and toss until evenly coated.
6. Spread the fries on the sheet pan in a single layer, not touching.
7. Bake for 15 minutes, flip each fry, then bake 10-15 minutes more, until crispy and browned at the tips.
8. Sprinkle with salt and let rest for 5 minutes before serving.

## Notes ##

- Adapted from [Gimme Some Oven's baked sweet potato fries](https://www.gimmesomeoven.com/oven-roasted-sweet-potato-fries/), swapping the smoked paprika for Szechuan peppercorns.
- MacroFactor: about 137 kcal per serving.
- ⚠️ = amount estimated from MacroFactor's nutrition totals (the export didn't include real amounts). Please double-check.
