# Roasted Broccoli #

Makes About 4 Servings

## Ingredients ##

- 1 lb 2 oz (510 g) Broccoli Florets (petite florets work well)
- 1 T (12 g) Olive Oil
- Salt and Pepper

## Instructions ##

1. Preheat oven (or air fryer) to 400°F.
2. Toss broccoli with olive oil, salt, and pepper and spread out on a sheet pan in a single layer.
3. Roast for about 15 minutes, stirring partway through, until the edges are browned and crispy.
