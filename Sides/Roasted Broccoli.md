# Roasted Broccoli #

Makes About 4 Servings

## Ingredients ##

- 1 lb 2 oz (510 g) Broccoli Florets (petite florets work well)
- 1 T (12 g) Olive Oil
- Salt and Pepper

## Instructions ##

1. Preheat oven (or air fryer) to 425°F ⚠️.
2. Toss broccoli with olive oil, salt, and pepper and spread out on a sheet pan in a single layer.
3. Roast for 15-20 minutes, stirring halfway, until the edges are browned and crispy ⚠️.
