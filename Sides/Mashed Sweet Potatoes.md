# Mashed Sweet Potatoes #

Makes About 8 Servings

## Ingredients ##

- 4 1/2 lb (2085 g) Sweet Potatoes, peeled and cubed
- 3 T Olive Oil
- 2/3 c Shallot, minced (about 2 large)
- 3 Cloves Garlic, minced
- 2/3 c (150 g) Greek Yogurt
- Salt and Pepper to Taste

## Instructions ##

1. Add sweet potatoes to a large pot, cover with cold salted water, and bring to a boil. Cook for 15-20 minutes, until very tender, then drain.
2. While the sweet potatoes cook, heat olive oil in a small pan over medium-low heat. Add shallot and garlic and cook, stirring, for 2-3 minutes, until soft and fragrant.
3. Return sweet potatoes to the pot and add the shallot mixture and Greek yogurt.
4. Mash until smooth and season with salt and pepper to taste.

## Notes ##

- MacroFactor: about 298 kcal and 6 g protein per serving.
