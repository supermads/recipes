# Bean Pasta #

Makes About 8 Servings

## Ingredients ##

- 1 Batch [Crock Pot Beans](Crock%20Pot%20Beans.md)
- 1 lb Small Pasta (fusilli, mini farfalle, etc.)
- Salt, for the pasta water

## Instructions ##

1. Make a batch of [Crock Pot Beans](Crock%20Pot%20Beans.md).
2. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta according to package instructions.
3. Scoop out about 1 c of the pasta water, then drain the pasta.
4. Stir the pasta into the beans. Add a splash of pasta water at a time until it's as saucy as you like.
5. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

## Notes ##

- MacroFactor: about 480 kcal and 21 g protein per serving. The numbers change a little every batch depending on which pasta you use, so swap the pasta ingredient in MacroFactor's "Bean Pasta" recipe to match the box.
