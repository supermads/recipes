# Orange Chicken and Cabbage #

Makes About 4 Servings

## Ingredients ##

- 1 1/3 lb (590 g) Boneless Skinless Chicken Thighs
- 3 T (60 g) Orange Marmalade
- 1 T Soy Sauce, divided
- 3 Cloves Garlic, grated
- 1 T Fresh Ginger, grated
- 4 Large Green Onions, sliced
- 2 t Salt and 1/4 t Pepper
- 1 Medium Head Red Cabbage (about 840 g), chopped
- 1 T + 1 t Olive Oil, divided

## Instructions ##

1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
2. In a bowl, stir together marmalade, 1 T soy sauce, garlic, ginger, and most of the green onions. Season the chicken with salt and pepper and toss it in the sauce.
3. Toss the cabbage with 1 T olive oil and 1 T soy sauce and spread it on a sheet pan.
4. Lay the chicken on top of the cabbage and drizzle with the remaining olive oil.
5. Bake until the chicken is cooked through (165°F): about 25-30 minutes for boneless thighs ⚠️ or about 45 minutes for bone-in.
6. Top with the remaining green onions.
