# TVP Pie #

Makes 1 9" Pie (6 Servings)

## Filling ##

- 1 T Olive Oil
- 1 Large Onion, chopped
- 1 1/2 c Dry TVP ⚠️
- 1 t Better Than Bouillon Chicken Base + 1 1/2 c Hot Water ⚠️
- 1 15-oz Can Kidney Beans, drained and rinsed ⚠️
- 1 10-oz Package Frozen Mixed Vegetables
- 1 T Low Sodium Soy Sauce
- 1/2 c BBQ Sauce ⚠️
- 1 t Garlic Powder ⚠️
- Pinch of Salt

## Cornbread Topping ##

- 1 T Ground Flaxseed + 3 T Water ⚠️
- 3/4 c Medium Grind Cornmeal ⚠️
- 1/2 c White Whole Wheat Flour ⚠️
- 2 T Sugar ⚠️
- 1 T Baking Powder ⚠️
- Pinch of Salt
- 1 c Whole Milk ⚠️
- 1 T Olive Oil
- 2 T Butter, melted ⚠️

## Instructions ##

1. Preheat oven to 450°F and oil a 9" pie pan.
2. Stir the bouillon into the hot water and pour it over the TVP. Let it sit for 5-10 minutes, until soft.
3. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add onion and the soaked TVP and cook, stirring, until browned.
4. Add beans, frozen veggies, soy sauce, BBQ sauce, garlic powder, and salt. Simmer for about 15 minutes, then pour into the pie pan.
5. For the topping, stir flaxseed and water together and let it sit for 5 minutes. Whisk cornmeal, flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a bowl, then stir in milk, olive oil, melted butter, and the flax mixture until just combined.
6. Pour the cornbread batter over the filling and spread it to the edges.
7. Bake for about 20 minutes, until the cornbread is golden and a toothpick comes out clean.

## Notes ##

- Adapted from the "Chili Pie" on the Cooking the Books YouTube channel. Changes: TVP instead of ground meat, BBQ sauce, and homemade cornbread instead of a boxed mix.
- MacroFactor: about 428 kcal and 24 g protein per serving.
- ⚠️ = amount estimated from MacroFactor's nutrition totals (the export didn't include real amounts). Please double-check.
