# Vegetable Stir Fry #

Makes About 4 Servings

## Sauce ##

- 1 c Vegetable Stock
- 2 T Soy Sauce ⚠️
- 1 T Rice Vinegar
- 1 T Sesame Oil
- 1 T Sugar ⚠️
- 1 T Cornstarch
- Pinch of Black Pepper

## Stir Fry ##

- 2 T Grapeseed Oil ⚠️
- 1 Onion, sliced
- 2 Carrots, thinly sliced ⚠️
- 4 oz Shiitake Mushrooms, sliced ⚠️
- 1 T Fresh Ginger, minced ⚠️
- 3 Cloves Garlic, minced ⚠️
- 2 c Red Cabbage, sliced ⚠️
- 2 c Snow Peas ⚠️
- 1 T Toasted Sesame Seeds

## Instructions ##

1. Whisk all sauce ingredients together in a small bowl and set aside.
2. Heat grapeseed oil in a large pan or wok over medium-high heat.
3. Add onion and carrots and cook, stirring, for 3-4 minutes, until starting to soften.
4. Add mushrooms and cook for another 2-3 minutes.
5. Add ginger and garlic and cook, stirring, for about 30 seconds, until fragrant.
6. Add cabbage and snow peas and cook for 2-3 minutes, until bright and crisp-tender.
7. Give the sauce a stir, pour it in, and cook for 1-2 minutes, stirring, until thick and glossy.
8. Top with sesame seeds and serve over rice.

- Adapted from *The Official Stardew Valley Cookbook*. The method above is written from scratch.
