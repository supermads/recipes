# Teriyaki Chicken Thighs #

Makes about 12 Servings (3 Bags of 4 Servings or 2 Bags of 6 Servings)

## Ingredients ##

- ¾ c Low Sodium Soy Sauce
- ½ c Mirin
- ½ c Brown Sugar
- 2 T Rice Vinegar
- 1 T Sesame Oil
- 5 cloves Garlic, minced
- 1 inch piece of Ginger, minced (about 1 T)
- 1 T Corn Starch
- ⅓ c + 1 T Water, divided
- 3 ½-4 lbs Boneless Skinless Chicken Thighs
- Olive Oil
- Sesame Seeds and/or Scallions for Serving (optional)

## Instructions ##

1. Combine the soy sauce, mirin, brown sugar, rice vinegar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, and ⅓ c water in a medium sauce pan.
2. Stir 1 T water into the corn starch and stir it into the sauce mixture.
3. Bring the sauce to a boil over medium-medium high heat, stirring regularly so the sugar doesn't stick to the bottom of the pan.
4. Cover the sauce and reduce heat to low then simmer for about 15 minutes.
5. Remove sauce from the heat and let cool completely.
6. Divide sauce and chicken thighs into 2-3 freezer safe bags or containers and make sure the sauce covers the chicken completely.
7. Let the chicken marinate for a few hours in the fridge, or freeze and thaw in the fridge for about a day before cooking.
8. To cook the chicken:
    - Heat a large pan over medium heat and add a small drizzle of olive oil (If cooking 6 servings, you will probably need 2 pans).
    - Remove chicken thighs from the sauce and place topside down in the pan. Reserve the sauce.
    - Cook chicken for about 4-5 minutes a side, or until browned and cooked through. Remove the chicken from the pan and let it rest while you cook the reserved sauce.
    - Add the sauce to the pan used to cook the chicken and heat on low, stirring frequently, until it's simmering and any scraps of chicken in it are cooked through.
9. Slice or chop the chicken then serve with some of the sauce. I like to top it with sesame seeds and scallions and serve over rice with some roasted cabbage or broccoli.
