# BBQ TVP #

Makes About 4 Servings

## Ingredients ##

- 1 c (96 g) Dry TVP
- 1 1/2 t Chicken Base (like Better Than Bouillon) + 1 1/2 c Hot Water
- 1 T Olive Oil
- 1 Medium Onion, chopped
- 2 Cloves Garlic, minced
- 1 T Soy Sauce
- 1 c (264 g) BBQ Sauce (like Mastodon by KC Natural)
- Salt and Pepper to Taste

## Instructions ##

1. Stir the chicken base into the hot water, pour it over the TVP, and let it sit for 5-10 minutes, until the liquid is absorbed.
2. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add onion and cook for about 5 minutes, until soft. Add garlic and cook 1 more minute.
3. Add the TVP and soy sauce and cook for 3-4 minutes, stirring.
4. Stir in the BBQ sauce, lower the heat, and simmer for 10-15 minutes, stirring often, until thick. Add a splash of water if it gets too dry.
5. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

## Ways to Eat It ##

- **BBQ TVP Bowl:** 1 serving over 160 g (about 1 c) cooked jasmine rice, with 1 serving of [Roasted Cabbage](../Sides/Roasted%20Cabbage.md).
- **BBQ TVP Sweet Potatoes:** 1 serving stuffed into a baked sweet potato (250 g) with 1/2 t olive oil, 1 oz feta, and half a green onion (green part), sliced.
- On a bun with pickles.

## Notes ##

- Adapted from an online BBQ TVP recipe, using chicken base and soy sauce instead of vegetable broth, smoked paprika, and maple syrup.
- MacroFactor: about 199 kcal and 15 g protein per serving (BBQ TVP Bowl: about 473 kcal; BBQ TVP Sweet Potato: about 517 kcal).
- Use 1 t chicken base per cup of water.
