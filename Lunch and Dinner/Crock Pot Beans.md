# Crock Pot Beans #

Makes About 8 Servings

## Ingredients ##

- 1 lb (about 2 1/3 c) Dried Pinto Beans ⚠️
- 1 Large Onion, chopped
- 4 Cloves Garlic, minced ⚠️
- 2 T Olive Oil ⚠️
- 6 c Water ⚠️
- 2 t Better Than Bouillon Chicken Base and/or 1 T White Miso Paste ⚠️
- 1 T Ground Cumin
- 1 T Garlic Powder
- Salt and Pepper
- 2 T Butter ⚠️
- 1 Scoop Spinach Powder (optional)

## Instructions ##

1. Go through the beans and remove any rocks or moldy/gross beans, then rinse them.
2. Heat a crock pot on high. Add the beans, onion, garlic, olive oil, water, bouillon and/or miso, cumin, garlic powder, and a pinch of salt and pepper.
3. Cover and cook for 4-5 hours (longer if using older grocery store beans or some types of black beans), or until the beans have softened to your liking. About 3 hours into the cook, stir in a few big pinches of salt (about 1 T coarse salt, more or less to taste).
4. Add the butter and spinach powder (if using) and serve with rice or whatever you want!

## Notes ##

- Use these in [Bean Pasta](Bean%20Pasta.md), or mash them up for refried beans.
- MacroFactor: about 277 kcal and 13 g protein per serving.
