# Ginger Ale Syrup #

Makes About 1 1/4 c Syrup (6 Servings)

## Ingredients ##

- 1/2 c Fresh Ginger, thinly sliced (about a 4" piece) ⚠️
- 1 1/2 c Water ⚠️
- 1/2 c Sugar ⚠️
- 1/4 c Honey ⚠️
- 1 Lime, zested and juiced
- Seltzer, for serving

## Instructions ##

1. Add ginger, water, and sugar to a small saucepan and bring to a simmer over medium heat.
2. Simmer for 15-20 minutes, until slightly syrupy and very gingery.
3. Remove from heat and stir in the honey, lime zest, and lime juice.
4. Let cool, then strain out the ginger and store the syrup in a jar in the fridge.
5. To serve, add about 3 T of syrup to a glass of seltzer and stir. Adjust to taste.
