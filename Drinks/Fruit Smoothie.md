# Fruit Smoothie #

Makes 1 Smoothie

## Ingredients ##

- 2/3 c (150 g) Plain Nonfat Greek Yogurt
- 1/3 c (50 g) Fruit, fresh or frozen (strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, peaches, or mixed berries)
- 1/3 c (80 ml) Whole Milk
- 1 1/2 T (30 g) Maple Syrup

## Instructions ##

1. Add everything to a blender, starting with the milk and yogurt so the blades catch.
2. Blend until smooth, adding a splash more milk if it's too thick.

## Notes ##

- Covers Strawberry Smoothie (logged on 318 days, one of the most-logged foods), Peach Smoothie (56 days), Raspberry Smoothie (49 days), Berry Smoothie, and Blueberry Smoothie. All five come out to about 235-245 kcal with 18 g protein.
- MacroFactor confirms they're all this same base with 50 g of fruit. The Berry Smoothie splits it between mixed berries, raspberries, and blackberries.
