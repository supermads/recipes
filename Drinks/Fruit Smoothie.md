# Fruit Smoothie #

Makes 1 Smoothie

## Ingredients ##

- 2/3 c Plain Nonfat Greek Yogurt ⚠️
- 2/3-1 c Fruit, fresh or frozen (strawberries, blueberries, raspberries, or peaches) ⚠️
- 1/3 c Whole Milk ⚠️
- 1 T Maple Syrup ⚠️

## Instructions ##

1. Add everything to a blender, starting with the milk and yogurt so the blades catch.
2. Blend until smooth, adding a splash more milk if it's too thick.

## Notes ##

- Covers Strawberry Smoothie (logged on 318 days, one of the most-logged foods), Peach Smoothie (56 days), Raspberry Smoothie (49 days), and the "Blueberry Smoothie" MacroFactor recipe. All four come out to about 235-245 kcal with 18 g protein, so they look like the same base with a different fruit.
- Strawberries are lower calorie than blueberries, so the strawberry version probably uses closer to 1 c of fruit.
- ⚠️ = amount estimated from MacroFactor's nutrition totals (the export didn't include real amounts). Please double-check.
