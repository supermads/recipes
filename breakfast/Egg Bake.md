# Egg Bake #

Makes 4 Servings (12 Muffins or 1 Casserole)

## Ingredients ##

- 1 1/2 c Liquid Egg Whites ⚠️
- 1/2 c 2% Cottage Cheese ⚠️
- 1 c Bisquick ⚠️
- 2 1/2 c Sweet Potato, diced small (or leftover [Crispy Sweet Potatoes](../Sides/Crispy%20Sweet%20Potatoes.md)) ⚠️
- 3 Green Onions, sliced ⚠️
- 2 oz Part Skim Mozzarella, shredded ⚠️
- 2 T Grated Parmesan ⚠️
- Grapeseed or Olive Oil, for greasing

## Instructions ##

1. Preheat oven to 350°F and grease a muffin tin or casserole dish with oil.
2. Whisk egg whites until smooth, then add all other ingredients and stir to combine.
3. Pour into the muffin tin or casserole dish.
4. Bake at 350°F for 20-25 minutes (muffins) or 35-40 minutes (casserole), until the center is set.

## Variation: Higher Protein Egg Bakes ##

Swap the sweet potato for sautéed mushrooms and use a mix of whole eggs and egg whites. Bake in a water bath (set the ramekins or muffin tin on a rimmed sheet pan with about 1/4" of water) at 400°F, about 20 minutes for muffins or 30 minutes for ramekins, until puffed and set. This version is adapted from an online recipe for sausage, pepper, and cheese egg bakes.
