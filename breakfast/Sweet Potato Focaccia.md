# Sweet Potato Focaccia #

Makes 1 9x13" Pan (8 Slices)

## Ingredients ##

- 1 Sweet Potato, baked and mashed (about 1/2 c / 115 g) ⚠️
- 1 1/4 c (300 mL) Warm Water (85-95°F) ⚠️
- 1 Packet (2 1/4 t) Active Dry Yeast
- 3 1/4 c (390 g) White Whole Wheat Flour ⚠️
- 2 t Kosher Salt ⚠️
- 1/3 c Olive Oil, divided ⚠️
- Flaky salt, for the top (optional)

## Instructions ##

1. Stir the yeast into the warm water and let it sit for 5 minutes, until foamy.
2. Add mashed sweet potato, flour, salt, and 2 T olive oil and mix until a sticky dough forms. Knead in a stand mixer for 5-8 minutes, or by hand with wet hands.
3. Cover and let rise for 1-1 1/2 hours, until doubled.
4. Pour most of the remaining olive oil into a 9x13" pan. Turn the dough into the pan and gently stretch it toward the corners. Cover and let rise for another 30-45 minutes.
5. Preheat oven to 425°F.
6. Drizzle with the last of the oil, then dimple all over with oiled fingertips and sprinkle with flaky salt.
7. Bake for 20-25 minutes, until deep golden.

## Notes ##

- Adapted from [Justine Doiron's Sweet Potato Focaccia](https://justinesnacks.com/sweet-potato-focaccia/) (also in *Justine Cooks*), using white whole wheat flour instead of bread flour.
