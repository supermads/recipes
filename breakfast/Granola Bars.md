# Granola Bars #

Makes 8 Servings

## Ingredients ##

- 2 c Rolled Oats
- ½ c Raw Cashews
- ½ c Roasted Peanuts (I get lightly salted)
- ½ c Raisins
- ⅓ c Mini Chocolate Chips
- 3 T Flaxseed
- 3 T Hemp Hearts
- 1 t Cinnamon
- ½ c Peanut Butter
- ½ c Maple Syrup
- 1 t Vanilla Extract
- ¼ t Salt

## Instructions ##

1. Preheat oven to 320°F (If using Anova: add 25% steam or use pre-set Steam-Baked Chewy Granola Bar recipe).
2. Combine oats, cashews, peanuts, raisins, chocolate chips, flaxseed, hemp hearts, and cinnamon in a medium to large mixing bowl.
3. Add peanut butter and maple syrup to a microwave safe measuring cup or bowl and microwave for 30 seconds.
4. Stir vanilla and salt into the peanut butter mixture.
5. Pour the peanut butter mixture into the bowl of dry ingredients and mix well so that everything is coated.
6. Pour the mixture onto a parchment-lined baking sheet and spread it out until it's about ½ inch thick, or covers most of a 12x16 inch pan. Use a knife to divide the granola into 8 bars.
7. Bake at 320°F for about 20 min or until golden brown, optionally rotating the pan halfway through to get more even browning.
8. Carefully use the edges of the parchment to move the sheet of granola bars onto a cooling rack and let them cool undisturbed for about 1 hour.
9. Store the bars in an airtight container at room temp for up to 1 week or in the fridge for up to 2 weeks.
