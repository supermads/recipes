# Homemade Bisquick Pancakes #

Makes About 8 Pancakes

## Homemade Bisquick (Makes About 5 1/4 c, 6 Servings) ##

- 5 c (600 g) White Whole Wheat Flour
- 2 1/2 T Baking Powder
- 1 1/4 t Kosher Salt
- 5 T Olive Oil

1. Whisk together flour, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl.
2. Drizzle in the olive oil and mix with a fork or your fingers until there are no big clumps and it looks like damp sand.
3. Store in an airtight container. This mix is also used in the [Fruit Pudding Pie](../dessert/Fruit%20Pudding%20Pie.md).

## Pancake Ingredients ##

- 2 T Ground Flaxseed + 6 T Water
- 1 3/4 c (about 1/3 of a batch) Homemade Bisquick
- 1 c Whole Milk
- 1 T Olive Oil, plus more for the pan

## Instructions ##

1. Stir flaxseed and water together in a medium bowl and let it sit for about 5 minutes, until gloopy.
2. Add the Homemade Bisquick, milk, and olive oil and stir until just combined. A few lumps are fine.
3. Heat a pan or griddle over medium heat and lightly oil it.
4. Pour about 1/4 c of batter per pancake and cook until bubbles form on top and the edges look set, then flip and cook another 1-2 minutes.

## Variations ##

- **Maple Pancakes:** Add about 2 3/4 T (55 g) maple syrup per batch, either in the batter or drizzled on top.
- **Cinnamon Sugar Pancakes:** Stir 2 T (25 g) sugar and 1 t cinnamon into the batter. Makes about 9.
- **Sourdough Maple Pancakes:** About 4 batches of Maple Pancakes, plus sourdough starter stirred into the batter. Makes about 35. MacroFactor tracks the starter as 1/4 c (31 g) white whole wheat flour, the same way the [Bread Machine Sourdough](Bread%20Machine%20Sourdough.md) does.

## Notes ##
