# Bread Machine Sourdough #

Makes 1 1 1/2-lb Loaf (8 Servings)

## Ingredients ##

- 3/4 c Water
- 1 c Sourdough Starter (fed with white whole wheat flour)
- 1 1/2 t Salt
- 2 2/3 c White Whole Wheat Flour (or swap 1/3 c for hard red spring whole wheat flour)
- 1 1/2 t Active Dry Yeast

## Instructions ##

1. Add ingredients to the bread machine pan in the order listed.
2. Bake on the "Basic" setting (about 3 hours).
3. Let cool completely before slicing.

## Notes ##

- Adapted from [Sourdough Bread for the Bread Machine on Food.com](https://www.food.com/recipe/sourdough-bread-for-the-bread-machine-47089), using whole wheat flours instead of bread flour.
- This is the bread used for [French Toast](French%20Toast.md), Honey Toast, and sandwiches.
- MacroFactor counts the starter as about 1/2 c flour.
- Logged on 12 days (Sept-Oct 2026).
- MacroFactor: about 175 kcal and 9 g protein per serving.
