# French Toast #

Makes 3 Slices

## Ingredients ##

- 1 T Ground Flaxseed + 3 T Water ⚠️
- 1/3 c Whole Milk ⚠️
- 1/2 t Cinnamon ⚠️
- 1/2 t Vanilla Extract ⚠️
- 3 Thick Slices Sourdough Bread (from the bread machine) ⚠️
- Butter or oil, for the pan

## Instructions ##

1. Stir flaxseed and water together in a shallow bowl and let it sit for about 5 minutes, until gloopy.
2. Whisk in milk, cinnamon, and vanilla.
3. Heat a pan over medium heat and add a little butter or oil.
4. Dip each slice of bread in the mixture, flipping so both sides soak it up.
5. Cook for 2-3 minutes per side, until golden.

## Variation: Baked Egg White French Toast Cups ##

Whisk together 1 c liquid egg whites ⚠️, 1/2 c milk ⚠️, 1 T vanilla, 1 T cinnamon, 3 T maple syrup, and a pinch of salt. Toss in about 4 slices of cubed bread ⚠️ and let them soak for 3-5 minutes. Divide between a greased 12-cup muffin tin and bake at 375°F for 13-15 minutes, until golden and set. Makes 10-12 cups.
