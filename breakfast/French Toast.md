# French Toast #

Makes 3 Servings

## Ingredients ##

- 1 1/2 T Ground Flaxseed + 4 1/2 T Water
- 6 T Whole Milk
- 3/4 t Cinnamon
- 1 1/2 t Vanilla Extract
- 300 g Rustic Bread (about 6 slices of the Kirkland Rustic Italian Loaf)
- Butter or oil, for the pan

## Instructions ##

1. Stir flaxseed and water together in a shallow bowl and let it sit for about 5 minutes, until gloopy.
2. Whisk in milk, cinnamon, and vanilla.
3. Heat a pan over medium heat and add a little butter or oil.
4. Dip each slice of bread in the mixture, flipping so both sides soak it up.
5. Cook for 2-3 minutes per side, until golden.

## Variation: Baked Egg White French Toast Cups ##

Whisk together 1 1/4 c (300 g) liquid egg whites, 1/4 c milk, 1 t vanilla, 1 t cinnamon, 2 T maple syrup, and a pinch of salt. Toss in 300 g cubed [Bread Machine Sourdough](Bread%20Machine%20Sourdough.md) and let it soak for 3-5 minutes. Divide between a greased 12-cup muffin tin and bake at 375°F for 13-15 minutes, until golden and set. Makes 10 cups.

## Notes ##

- Merged from the "French Toast (Flax)" and "Egg White French Toast" MacroFactor recipes. "French Toast" was also logged on 26 days going back to 2022.
- MacroFactor: about 295 kcal and 10 g protein per serving (egg white cups: about 92 kcal and 6 g protein each).
