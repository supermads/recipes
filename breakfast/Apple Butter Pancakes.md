# Apple Butter Pancakes #

Makes About 13 Pancakes or 4 Waffles

## Ingredients ##

- 2 T Ground Flaxseed + 6 T Water ⚠️
- 2 c White Whole Wheat Flour ⚠️
- 2 T Brown Sugar ⚠️
- 1/4 c Dry Buttermilk Powder ⚠️
- 2 t Baking Powder ⚠️
- 1/2 t Baking Soda ⚠️
- 1 t Cinnamon ⚠️
- 1/2 t Kosher Salt ⚠️
- 1 1/2 c Water ⚠️
- 1 t Vanilla Extract ⚠️
- 1/4 c Apple Butter ⚠️
- 1/4 c Olive Oil, plus more for the pan ⚠️

## Instructions ##

1. Stir flaxseed and water together in a small bowl and let it sit for about 5 minutes, until gloopy.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, brown sugar, buttermilk powder, baking powder, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt.
3. Add water, vanilla, apple butter, olive oil, and the flax mixture and stir until just combined.
4. Heat a pan or griddle over medium heat and lightly oil it.
5. Pour about 1/4 c of batter per pancake and cook until bubbles form and the edges look set, then flip and cook another 1-2 minutes.

## Variation: Apple Butter Pebis Waffles ##

Swap the olive oil for the same amount of melted butter and whisk 1/4 c powdered peanut butter ⚠️ into the dry ingredients. Cook in a waffle iron. Makes about 4 big waffles.
