# Rice Pudding #

Makes About 6 Servings

## Ingredients ##

- 2/3 c Uncooked Jasmine Rice ⚠️
- 4 c Whole Milk ⚠️
- 1/2 c Sugar ⚠️
- 2 T Butter ⚠️
- 1 t Vanilla Extract

## Instructions ##

1. Add rice, milk, and sugar to a medium saucepan and bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat, stirring often so the bottom doesn't scorch.
2. Lower heat and simmer, stirring frequently, for 30-40 minutes ⚠️, until the rice is very soft and the pudding is thick and creamy.
3. Remove from heat and stir in the butter and vanilla.
4. Serve warm or chilled. It thickens a lot as it cools. Good with [Cherry Compote](../Sides/Cherry%20Compote.md).
