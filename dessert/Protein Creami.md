# Protein Creami #

Makes 1 Ninja Creami Pint (2 Servings)

## Chocolate ##

- 2 c (480 g) Chocolate Ultra-Filtered Milk (like Fairlife)
- 2 Scoops (60 g) Whey Protein Isolate

## PB Molasses ##

- 1 c (240 g) Whole Milk
- 1 Scoop (30 g) Unflavored Whey Protein
- 1/4 c (26 g) Powdered Peanut Butter
- 1 T (21 g) Molasses
- 2 T (40 g) Maple Syrup
- 1 1/2 t Vanilla Extract
- 3 Dashes Kosher Salt
- 1/16 t Cinnamon and 1/8 t Ginger (optional)

## Instructions ##

1. Whisk or blend everything for your flavor until the protein powder is fully dissolved.
2. Pour into a Ninja Creami pint and freeze overnight, on a level surface.
3. Spin on the Lite Ice Cream setting. If it's crumbly, add a splash of milk and re-spin.

## Notes ##

- Merged from MacroFactor's "Chocolate Protein Creami" (it was in the export twice) and "PB Molasses Ice Cream."
- MacroFactor: Chocolate is about 500 kcal and 76 g protein per pint. PB Molasses is about 589 kcal and 42 g protein per pint.
