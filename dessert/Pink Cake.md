# Pink Cake #

Makes 1 Layer Cake (16 Slices)

## Strawberry Watermelon Filling ##

- 1 c Strawberries, halved ⚠️
- __ Yellow Watermelon, cubed (a quarter melon?) ⚠️
- __ Sugar
- 1 t Lemon Zest
- Juice of 1 Lemon
- 1 T Cornstarch + __ Water

## Cake ##

- __ White Whole Wheat Flour
- __ Baking Powder
- __ Baking Soda
- __ Sugar
- __ Butter, softened
- 1 T Ground Flaxseed + 3 T Water
- __ Cultured Buttermilk Powder + __ Water
- __ Vanilla Extract

## Frosting ##

- __ Butter, softened
- __ Cream Cheese, softened
- __ Confectioners Sugar
- __ Freeze-Dried Strawberries, ground into powder

## Instructions ##

1. **Filling:** Simmer the strawberries, watermelon, sugar, lemon zest, and lemon juice until the fruit breaks down. Stir in the cornstarch slurry and cook for another minute or two, until thick. Cool completely.
2. **Cake:** Make the flax egg and let it sit for 5 minutes. Cream the butter and sugar, beat in the flax egg and vanilla, then alternate adding the dry ingredients and the buttermilk. Bake in two greased cake pans until a toothpick comes out clean. Cool completely.
3. **Frosting:** Beat butter and cream cheese until smooth, then beat in the confectioners sugar and strawberry powder.
4. **Assemble:** Spread filling between the layers, then frost the outside.
