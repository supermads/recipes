# Lemon Sorbet #

Makes About 10 Servings

## Ingredients ##

- 1 3/4 c (358 g) Sugar
- 1 3/4 c (424 g) Water
- 1 T + 1 t Lemon Zest
- 1 1/3 c (317 g) Fresh Lemon Juice (about 7 lemons)

## Instructions ##

1. Add sugar and water to a small saucepan over medium heat and stir until the sugar dissolves. You don't need to boil it.
2. Remove from heat, stir in the lemon zest, and let cool.
3. Stir in the lemon juice and chill in the fridge for at least a few hours or overnight.
4. Churn in an ice cream maker, or freeze in Ninja Creami pints and spin on the Sorbet setting.

## Variation: Lemon Maple Sorbet ##

Makes about 12 servings. Use 2 c (488 g) lemon juice, 2 c water, 1 T zest, 3/4 c + 2 T (168 g) sugar, and 3/4 c + 2 T (278 g) maple syrup. Stir the maple syrup in with the lemon juice.

## Notes ##

- Merged from two MacroFactor recipes: "Lemon Sorbet" and "Lemon Maple Sorbet."
- MacroFactor: about 146 kcal per serving (maple version: about 124 kcal).
