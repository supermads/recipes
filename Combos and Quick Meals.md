# Combos and Quick Meals #

These are quick assemblies of other recipes or a few store-bought things. They're saved in MacroFactor as recipes, but don't really need their own recipe pages.

## Sandwiches and Toast ##

- **Honey Toast:** A slice of bread machine sourdough, toasted, with butter and about 1 T honey. *(~275 kcal)*
    - **Honey Bread:** The same thing on a slice of rustic Italian loaf. *(~345 kcal)*
- **Pebis Jebis:** Peanut butter and apple butter on wheat bread. *(~410 kcal; the "half PB" version is ~333 kcal)*
    - **PB2 Pebis Jebis:** Powdered peanut butter and apple butter on whole grain and seed bread. *(~327 kcal, 18 g protein)*
- **Bacon Egg Sandwich:** Eggs scrambled with a splash of milk and a slice of thick-cut bacon on a toasted onion bun. *(~455 kcal, 25 g protein per sandwich)*
- **Chicken Sandwich:** BBQ tendies (chicken tenderloins baked with BBQ sauce) on a hoagie roll with shredded romaine, onion, and dijon. *(~376 kcal, 39 g protein)*
- **Yaki Sando:** Teriyaki Chicken Thighs and Roasted Cabbage on a hoagie roll with onion. *(~484 kcal, 32 g protein)*
- **Muffin With Fixins:** *Mads, what goes on this one?* Logged on 40 days (2023-2024). *(~483 kcal, 19 g protein)*

## Bowls ##

- **Teriyaki Bowl:** Teriyaki Chicken Thighs or crispy tofu with teriyaki sauce over jasmine rice with Roasted Cabbage. Logged on 38 days. *(~527 kcal, 32 g protein with chicken)*
    - **Teriyaki Tofu Bowl:** Extra firm tofu pan-fried in olive oil, tossed in teriyaki sauce, with rice and Roasted Cabbage. *(~495 kcal, 24 g protein)*
- **Teriyaki Chicken and Fried Rice:** Teriyaki Chicken Thighs with [Fried Rice](Lunch%20and%20Dinner/Fried%20Rice.md). *(~504 kcal, 37 g protein)*
- **Tofu Fried Rice:** [Fried Rice](Lunch%20and%20Dinner/Fried%20Rice.md) using [Crispy Tofu](Lunch%20and%20Dinner/Crispy%20Tofu%20with%20Sweet%20Soy%20Sauce.md) as the protein. Logged on 30 days. *(~730 kcal, 28 g protein)*
- **BBQ TVP Bowl** and **BBQ TVP Sweet Potatoes:** See the BBQ TVP recipe.

## Other ##

- **Quesadilla:** Part skim mozzarella in a whole wheat tortilla, toasted in a pan. *(~285 kcal)*
- **Pesto Pasta:** Macaroni tossed with pesto. *(~416 kcal, 11 g protein)*
- **Small Janie Salad:** Shredded romaine with plant-based bacon bits and a dressing of dijon, capers, lemon juice, maple syrup, and olive oil. *(~165 kcal)*
- **Cookie Tofu Brownies:** Tofu Brownies (see *Tried Once*) with cookie dough dolloped on top before baking. *(~199 kcal)*
