# Tried Once #

These recipes were made once or twice (or saved and never logged). Ingredients come from MacroFactor, but **the export didn't include real amounts**. If any of these are keepers, give them their own recipe page.

## Lunch and Dinner ##

- [ ] **Bulk Burritos** *(logged 3 days, Feb 2026; ~570 kcal, 23 g protein each, makes 6)*: olive oil, onion, garlic, black beans, kidney beans, garbanzo beans, cumin, spinach powder, jasmine rice, part skim mozzarella, 10" flour tortillas. Looks like a freezer burrito, similar to the [Chicken Tacos](Lunch%20and%20Dinner/Chicken%20Tacos.md).
- [ ] **Lazy Pad See Ew** *(1 day, Feb 2026; ~660 kcal, 34 g protein, makes 2)*: chicken thighs, rice noodles, egg, soy sauce, molasses, sugar, shredded cabbage, olive oil, garlic, water.
- [ ] **Chestnut Soup** *(4 days, Mar-Apr 2026; ~295 kcal, makes 4)*: cooked chestnuts, onion, celery, olive oil, chicken base, water, whole milk, heavy cream.
- [ ] **BBQ Pizza** *(1 day, Oct 2026; ~427 kcal, 20 g protein, makes 6)*: pourable pizza dough, BBQ sauce, sweet onion, olive oil, low moisture mozzarella.
- [ ] **TVP Burgers** *(never logged; ~489 kcal, 32 g protein, makes 6)*: flax egg, TVP, chicken base, nutritional yeast, miso, onion powder, garlic powder, BBQ sauce, egg whites, soy sauce, white whole wheat flour, quick oats, olive oil. Served on sourdough with mozzarella, dijon, onion, and romaine.
- [ ] **Sweet Potato Hash** *(never logged; ~78 kcal, makes 8)*: sweet potato cubes, onion, olive oil.

## Breakfast ##

- [ ] **Cake Mix Pancakes** *(2 days, Apr 2026; ~154 kcal, makes 12)*: triple chocolate fudge cake mix, whole milk, flax egg.
- [ ] **Plant Protein Pancakes** *(6 days, Jun 2026; ~166 kcal, 9 g protein, makes 12)*: complete pancake and waffle mix, whole milk, mini chocolate chips.
- [ ] **Gluten Free Protein Pancakes** *(3 days, Aug 2026; ~277 kcal, 12 g protein, makes 12)*: GF protein pancake mix, egg, egg white, butter, whole milk, blueberries.
- [ ] **Krusteaz Muffins** *(4 days, Sept 2026; ~171 kcal, makes 12)*: Krusteaz cinnamon swirl mix made with grapeseed oil, a flax egg plus 1 egg, and blueberries. There was also a never-logged version following the box (water, butter, egg).

## Desserts ##

- [ ] **PB Ice Cream Pie** *(1 day for the chocolate coffee version, Jul 2026; ~426-469 kcal a slice, makes 6)*: Crust is graham crackers (or Reese's chocolate sandwich cookies), butter, and sugar. The PB layer is peanut butter, heavy cream, powdered sugar, and gelatin bloomed in water. Topped with homemade ice cream:
    - **Vanilla Ice Cream:** cream, whole milk, sugar, vanilla, kosher salt
    - **Chocolate Coffee Ice Cream:** the vanilla base plus instant espresso powder and cocoa powder
- [ ] **Tofu Brownies** *(1 day, Sept 2026; ~168 kcal, makes 16)*: boxed brownie mix with silken tofu in place of the eggs and oil.
- [ ] **Rice Crispie Treats** *(2 days, Feb 2026; ~304 kcal, makes 8)*: cocoa rice cereal, mini marshmallows, chocolate fudge, olive oil, kosher salt.
